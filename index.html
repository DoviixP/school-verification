Admissions

Admission formalities

Documents and requirements

  • Photocopy of birth certificate
  • Completed and signed admission form (available from the secretariat)
  • Report card from the previous class
  • One ream of A4 paper (all students)
  • Registration fees

Additional information

  • Reduction in fees for parents with three or more registered students, according to the school flyer.
  • The first 100 enrolled students benefit from a scholarship, according to the flyer.
  • Free school uniform and T-shirt are advertised by the school.

Admission offers and conditions may change. Contact the school to confirm current availability and terms.
